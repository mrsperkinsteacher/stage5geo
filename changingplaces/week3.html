LESSON 7

Where Do Australians Live and Why?

Learning intention: we are learning the difference between population size and population density, and why Australians live where they do.

External links

  • ABS - abs.gov.au (current population clock and statistics)
  • YouTube: “Why 95% of Australia is empty” (RealLifeLore or similar)

Christian worldview - Caretaking Earth

“He determined the times set for them and the exact places where they should live.”

Acts 17:26

Where people live is shaped by the creation God made - rainfall, rivers, soil. Discuss: is Australia “empty”, or is it full in a different way?

LESSON 8

First Nations Map of Australia

Learning intention: we are learning that Australia has always been a continent of hundreds of nations, and that displacement is part of the story of Australia’s changing places.

External links

  • AIATSIS Map of Indigenous Australia - aiatsis.gov.au/explore/map-indigenous-australia
  • Common Ground - commonground.org.au

Christian worldview - Embracing Diversity

“He has shown you, O mortal, what is good… to act justly and to love mercy and to walk humbly with your God.”

Micah 6:8

Justice begins with truth-telling. Discuss: why does knowing whose Country we are on matter to acting justly?

LESSON 9

A Sustainable Australia

Learning intention: we are learning how Australia’s population is projected to grow and how communities can grow sustainably.

External links

  • Narara Eco-Village - nararaecovillage.com
  • ABS population projections - abs.gov.au
  • Our World in Data - ourworldindata.org

Christian worldview - Imagining Innovations

“The earth is the Lord’s, and everything in it.”

Psalm 24:1

Narara Eco-Village is an attempt to build a community that treads lightly on what belongs to God. Discuss: what would your ideal “stewardship suburb” include?
