What is Urbanisation?

Learning intention: we are learning what urbanisation is and how urban and rural places differ.

Christian worldview - Embracing Diversity

“Go from your country, your people and your father’s household to the land I will show you.”

Genesis 12:1

Migration stories run right through Scripture - Abraham left everything familiar at God’s call. Discuss: what would make a family today leave the place they know?

LESSON 2

Push and Pull Factors

Learning intention: we are learning to explain why people move, using push factors (reasons to leave) and pull factors (reasons to arrive).

Christian worldview - Embracing Diversity

“Where you go I will go, and where you stay I will stay.”

Ruth 1:16

Naomi and Ruth’s move was driven by famine (push) and family (pull). Discuss: push and pull factors are ancient - what were the factors in Ruth’s decision?

LESSON 3

Three Causes of Urban Growth

Learning intention: we are learning the three processes that make cities grow: rural - urban migration, international migration, and natural increase.

Christian worldview - Caretaking Earth

“Seek the peace and prosperity of the city to which I have carried you… because if it prospers, you too will prosper.”

Jeremiah 29:7

God’s people were told to seek their city’s flourishing. Discuss: what would it look like to “seek the prosperity” of a rapidly growing city?
