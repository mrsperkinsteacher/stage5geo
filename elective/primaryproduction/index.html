"How do we feed 8 billion people? And what happens when the land, water or climate can no longer keep up?"
Agriculture, aquaculture, forestry and mining shape landscapes and feed the world. Australia is a major food exporter - understanding how primary industries work, and where they are under pressure, matters enormously.
