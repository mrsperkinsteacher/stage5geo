"The ocean covers 71% of Earth and only about 29% of the seafloor has been mapped in detail. What's down there?"

The ocean regulates our climate, feeds billions of people and is under unprecedented pressure from human activity. Australia has one of the world's longest coastlines - this topic is literally on our doorstep.
