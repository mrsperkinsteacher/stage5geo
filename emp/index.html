Environmental Change and Management

How do people manage environments that are changing — and who decides what "management" means?

About this unit. Students investigate how environments change over time and evaluate the different approaches used to manage and protect them. The unit builds towards each student completing an independent Environmental Management Plan (EMP) for a real local environmental issue on the Central Coast — collecting their own primary data on site, researching secondary data, and evaluating management options against economic, social and environmental criteria. Aboriginal Custodianship of Country is a required part of every student's plan, not an optional extra.
