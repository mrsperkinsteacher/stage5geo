Environmental Change and Management

How do people manage environments that are changing - and who decides what "management" means?

Heads up: this website is set up for Year 9, 2026, and some components of the assessment differ from the Year 10, 2026 assessment. Year 10 students, feel free to use this site to help you too, but as always, your school Assessment Task and Marking Guide are the final say on what's expected in your submission.
About this unit. In this unit, you'll explore how environments change over time and look at the different ways people manage and protect them. Everything builds towards your own independent Environmental Management Plan (EMP) - a real report on a real environmental issue somewhere on the Central Coast. You'll choose your own site, collect your own data out in the field, dig into research others have already done, and weigh up management options against what's affordable, what's fair, and what's best for the environment. Aboriginal Custodianship of Country is a core part of your plan, not an add-on - every EMP needs to show how Aboriginal knowledge and practice support caring for that place.
