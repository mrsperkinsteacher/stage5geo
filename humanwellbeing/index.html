Stage 5 Geography | Year 9

Human Wellbeing

How can human wellbeing and development be measured and explained?

← Back to Year 9 & 10 Geography home

About this unit. In this unit, you'll investigate what wellbeing really means, and why two countries with similar wealth can have very different lives. You'll learn to measure and map wellbeing using real indicators - GDP, GNI, HDI and more - read choropleth maps using the PQE method (Pattern, Quantify, Exception), and dig into why wellbeing varies so much between places, and within them. Along the way you'll fact-check AI tools against reliable sources, and look at real initiatives - from Aboriginal Community Controlled Organisations to global NGOs - working to improve wellbeing today.
Big questions this term: What makes human wellbeing a geographical issue? How can human wellbeing and development be measured and explained?
