📋 Summary of Lesson

This lesson started with education. The Human Development Index combines three dimensions: a long and healthy life (life expectancy), knowledge (mean years of schooling and expected years of schooling) and standard of living (GNI per capita). Education gets its own dimension because it shapes almost everything else - income, health choices and opportunities. Photos of classrooms from Haiti to Kenya to Bangladesh showed how different school can look.

Then you compared quantitative indicators (things you can count, like GNI or literacy rate) with qualitative ones (things that are harder to count, like freedom of speech or safety). You looked at the Happy Planet Index, Bhutan's Gross National Happiness, and compared countries' press freedom with their HDI rank.

📖 Glossary

Quantitative indicator - a measure you can count with numbers, e.g. GNI, literacy rate, life expectancy.
Qualitative indicator - a measure that's harder to capture with numbers, from surveys not counts, e.g. freedom of speech, safety.
Empowerment - having the knowledge, resources and opportunity to improve your own life.
Mean years of schooling - how many years of school adults have actually completed, on average.
Expected years of schooling - how many years of school a child starting today is expected to complete.
Happy Planet Index (HPI) - rates countries on long, happy and sustainable lives, using life expectancy, life satisfaction and ecological footprint.
Gross National Happiness (GNH) - Bhutan's way of measuring national progress by wellbeing, not just money.

🌍 Link to the SDGs

SDG 4 - Quality Education

Education is one of the three dimensions of the HDI, and this goal aims for quality education for every child.
SDG 16 - Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions

Freedom of speech and a free press are qualitative indicators of how fair and safe a country's institutions are.

The United Nations' 17 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) are the world's shared plan for improving wellbeing everywhere by 2030.

💡 Did You Know?

In the 2026 Happy Planet Index, Australia ranked 103rd of 147 countries - 3rd for life expectancy and 19th for wellbeing, but only 131st for ecological footprint. A country can do well on some parts of wellbeing and badly on others, which a single number like GDP would hide.
