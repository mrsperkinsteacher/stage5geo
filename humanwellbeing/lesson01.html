📋 Summary of Lesson

This lesson opened the Human Wellbeing unit. You learned that wellbeing means having what you need for life to be good - more than just income or wealth. Since the early 2000s, a global movement has pushed for measures of progress that go beyond a country's income, but countries are still most often compared using one economic number: GDP.

You met STEEP, the five domains geographers use to sort wellbeing indicators: Social, Technological, Environmental, Economic and Political. You sorted 15 real indicators into them, then read three people's quotes that showed wellbeing looks different depending on who you ask - for one person it starts with safety and food, for another it is about relationships, and for a third it is tied to land that is changing.

📖 Glossary

Wellbeing - having what you need for life to be good - more than just income or wealth.
STEEP - the five domains used to sort wellbeing indicators: Social, Technological, Environmental, Economic, Political.
GDP (Gross Domestic Product) - the most common, but limited, economic measure used to compare countries.
Indicator - a measure used to track something, such as literacy rate, life expectancy or CO2 emissions.
Development - once mainly about economic gain; today it also covers basic needs, equity, sustainability, freedom and safety.

🌍 Link to the SDGs

SDG 6 - Clean Water and Sanitation

Today's Geo Spark fact - about 1 in 4 people without safely managed drinking water - is exactly what this goal is working to fix.
SDG 3 - Good Health and Well-being

The UN goal named directly after wellbeing: healthy lives and wellbeing for everyone, at every age.

The United Nations' 17 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) are the world's shared plan for improving wellbeing everywhere by 2030.

💡 Did You Know?

Around 1 in 4 people worldwide - about 26 out of every 100 - do not have access to safely managed drinking water. How close was your Geo Spark guess? Clean water touches almost every STEEP domain at once: health, school attendance, work and the environment.

💼 Careers Connected to This Lesson

Wellbeing researcher - designs surveys and indexes, like the OECD Better Life Index, deciding which indicators actually capture a good life.

Water and sanitation engineer - plans and builds the systems that bring safe water to communities that don't have it yet.

Statistician (UN Statistics Division) - collects and checks the country data you can explore on UNdata.
